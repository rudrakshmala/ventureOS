**Business Requirements Document (BRD) for Pet Food Delivery App**

**Introduction:**
The purpose of this document is to outline the requirements for a simple mobile application that delivers pet food along with a navigation system. The application should be user-friendly, efficient, and provide a seamless experience for pet owners.

**Product Overview:**
The Pet Food Delivery App will allow users to order pet food and have it delivered to their location. The application will include a navigation system to enable real-time tracking of delivery personnel.

**Functional Requirements:**

1. **User Registration and Login**: Users should be able to register and log in to the application using their email address, phone number, or social media accounts.
2. **Pet Food Catalog**: The application should display a catalog of available pet food products, including product descriptions, prices, and images.
3. **Order Placement**: Users should be able to select products, add them to a cart, and place orders.
4. **Payment Gateway**: The application should integrate a secure payment gateway to facilitate online payments.
5. **Navigation System**: The application should include a navigation system that enables real-time tracking of delivery personnel.
6. **Order Tracking**: Users should be able to track the status of their orders, including estimated delivery time and location.
7. **Push Notifications**: The application should send push notifications to users when their order is confirmed, when the delivery person is on the way, and when the order is delivered.

**Non-Functional Requirements:**

1. **Performance**: The application should be able to handle a minimum of 100 concurrent users without significant performance degradation.
2. **Security**: The application should comply with industry standards for security, including encryption of sensitive data and secure payment processing.
3. **Usability**: The application should be user-friendly and easy to navigate, with clear and concise instructions and minimal cognitive load.
4. **Scalability**: The application should be designed to scale to meet increasing demand, with the ability to add or remove servers as needed.

**Assumptions and Dependencies:**

1. **Third-Party Integration**: The application will integrate with third-party services, including payment gateways and mapping services.
2. **Data Storage**: The application will store user data, order history, and product information in a secure database.

**Timeline and Milestones:**
The development of the Pet Food Delivery App is expected to take approximately 10 weeks, with the following milestones:

1. **Week 1-2**: Requirements gathering and planning
2. **Week 3-4**: Design and prototyping
3. **Week 5-6**: Development and testing
4. **Week 7-8**: Deployment and quality assurance
5. **Week 9-10**: Launch and post-launch review

**Risks and Mitigation:**

1. **Technical Risks**: Technical difficulties, such as integration issues with third-party services, may impact the development timeline. Mitigation: Identify potential risks and develop contingency plans.
2. **Market Risks**: Changes in market conditions, such as increased competition, may impact the application's success. Mitigation: Continuously monitor market trends and adjust the application's features and marketing strategy accordingly.

**Conclusion:**
The Pet Food Delivery App will provide a convenient and user-friendly way for pet owners to order pet food and have it delivered to their location. The application's navigation system will enable real-time tracking of delivery personnel, ensuring a seamless and efficient experience for users. With careful planning, development, and testing, the application is expected to be launched within the designated timeline and budget.
