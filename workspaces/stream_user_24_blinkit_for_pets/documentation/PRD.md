Business Requirements Document (BRD) for Pet Food Delivery App with Navigation System

Introduction:
The purpose of this document is to outline the requirements for a simple 10-minute app designed to deliver pet food along with a navigation system. The app aims to provide a convenient and efficient way for pet owners to order and receive pet food, while also facilitating navigation to the delivery location.

Product Vision:
The pet food delivery app will be a user-friendly, location-based platform that enables pet owners to order pet food and have it delivered to their doorstep. The app will feature a navigation system, allowing users to track the delivery in real-time and receive updates on the estimated arrival time.

Functional Requirements:

1. User Registration: The app will allow users to register and create an account, providing basic information such as name, email address, and phone number.
2. Pet Food Catalog: The app will display a catalog of available pet food products, including product descriptions, prices, and images.
3. Order Placement: Users will be able to place orders for pet food, selecting the desired products and quantities.
4. Payment Gateway: The app will integrate a secure payment gateway, allowing users to make payments online.
5. Navigation System: The app will feature a navigation system, using GPS technology to track the delivery location and provide real-time updates to the user.
6. Order Tracking: Users will be able to track the status of their orders, including the estimated delivery time and location.
7. Push Notifications: The app will send push notifications to users, updating them on the status of their orders and alerting them when the delivery is near.

Non-Functional Requirements:

1. Performance: The app will be designed to load quickly, with an average load time of less than 3 seconds.
2. Security: The app will ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of user data, using industry-standard security protocols such as SSL encryption.
3. Usability: The app will be designed with a user-friendly interface, making it easy for users to navigate and place orders.
4. Compatibility: The app will be compatible with both Android and iOS devices, as well as desktop computers.

System Requirements:

1. Front-end: The app will be built using a responsive design framework, such as React Native or Flutter.
2. Back-end: The app will be built using a server-side programming language, such as Node.js or Python.
3. Database: The app will use a relational database management system, such as MySQL or PostgreSQL.
4. Navigation System: The app will use a mapping service, such as Google Maps or Mapbox, to provide navigation and location-based services.

Assumptions and Dependencies:

1. The app will rely on a third-party payment gateway to process transactions.
2. The app will require access to the user's location, using GPS technology to track the delivery location.
3. The app will require a stable internet connection to function properly.

Acceptance Criteria:

1. The app will be able to handle a minimum of 100 concurrent users.
2. The app will be able to process orders and payments successfully.
3. The app will be able to provide real-time updates on the delivery location and estimated arrival time.
4. The app will be able to send push notifications to users, updating them on the status of their orders.

Timeline:
The estimated development time for the app is 10 minutes, with the following milestones:

1. User registration and login functionality (2 minutes)
2. Pet food catalog and order placement functionality (3 minutes)
3. Payment gateway integration and navigation system (3 minutes)
4. Testing and debugging (2 minutes)

Budget:
The estimated budget for the app is $100, which will cover the costs of development, testing, and deployment.

Conclusion:
The pet food delivery app with navigation system will provide a convenient and efficient way for pet owners to order and receive pet food. The app will feature a user-friendly interface, secure payment gateway, and real-time navigation system, making it a valuable tool for pet owners.
